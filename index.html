Frequently Asked Legal Questions

What should I do immediately if an FIR is registered against me in Etawah?

Ghabraye nahi. Do not give any written statement to the police without your lawyer present. Immediately contact a criminal lawyer to apply for Anticipatory Bail to prevent arrest.

Etawah District Court mein bail aane mein kitna time lagta hai?

Bail ka time crime ki severity par depend karta hai. Regular cases mein 2 se 4 din lag sakte hain. Humari team koshish karti hai ki strong arguments ke through first hearing mein hi relief mil jaye.

Do you handle cases in Allahabad High Court?

Yes, Advocate Vikas Yadav provides complete legal assistance from the District Sessions Court in Etawah all the way up to the Allahabad High Court for appeals and FIR Quashing.
